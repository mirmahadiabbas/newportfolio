AI Meeting MinutesLive

Paste a transcript, get back clean minutes: summary, decisions, actions with owners, and open questions, as a PDF in your inbox. Free to use.

Try it

Free, no sign-up. The form is at the bottom of this page, with sample data if you have no transcript to hand.

How it works for you

1Fill a short form: name, email, meeting title, date, attendees if you have them, and the transcript pasted as text. Up to roughly 45,000 characters, about an hour of meeting.
2The service reads the transcript and builds structured minutes from what was actually said.
3An email lands in your inbox, usually within a few minutes.
4The email body holds a short narrative summary and the action list; the full minutes come attached as a PDF.

What lands in your inbox

Email body

A 100 to 160 word narrative summary of the meeting, followed by the action list so owners can see their tasks without opening anything.

Attached PDF, five sections
  • Overview
  • Key discussion points, grouped by topic
  • Decisions
  • Actions: owner, task, deadline, priority
  • Open questions

The pipeline behind it

WebhookThis page's form posts straight to the scenario
Make.comSix-module scenario orchestrates the flow
Claude APIStructured prompt returns strict JSON: summary, decisions, actions, questions
Google DocsBranded minutes template auto-fills from the JSON
PDFDocument exports as a PDF
GmailSummary email sends with the PDF attached

Built not to bluff

  • Extracts only what is in the transcript; never invents names, deadlines, decisions, or numbers.
  • Distinguishes decisions that were explicitly agreed from suggestions that were merely raised.
  • Actions get an owner only when the transcript makes ownership clear; otherwise they are marked Unassigned.
  • Missing deadlines are marked "Not specified" rather than guessed.
  • Non-transcript or too-thin input fails gracefully: the reply explains the problem instead of fabricating minutes.

Honest limits

Paste-only text transcripts for now English output, British English style Free to use, capped monthly volume
